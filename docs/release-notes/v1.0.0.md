The first release of MIL-C: cytometry from a folder of `.fcs` files and the FlowJo workspace
that gates them, to balanced concatenation, UMAP, tSNE, FlowSOM and PhenoGraph, statistics
between groups and figures that can be published. Open source, no R needed.

## Download

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS (Apple Silicon) | `MIL-C-1.0.0-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| Windows (x64) | `MIL-C-1.0.0-windows-x64.msi`, or the portable `.zip` beside it |
| Linux (x86-64, Ubuntu 22.04 or as recent) | `.deb` for Debian, Ubuntu and Mint; `.tar.gz` for any other |

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksum of each file. **The installers are not signed**: the first
time, on macOS right-click the application ▸ Open (or System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸
Open Anyway); on Windows, "More info" ▸ "Run anyway". Every installer is built and its
self-test run by the CI on a clean machine of its system; `MIL-C selftest` runs it again on
yours.

## What is in it

**Samples.** Folders and FlowJo workspaces (`.wsp`): gates, compensation and scales read from
the workspace, and given to the files it does not list when the listed ones share them. A
table of your own columns, filled by hand or from the file names. Batch normalisation in the
manner of CytoNorm. Quality control of each acquisition in the order it was acquired — clogs,
steps, drifts and short shifts — removed as a first population, Clean. Three ways to balance
the samples before they are concatenated, with the sampling plan shown first.

**Gates.** Rectangle, polygon, freehand lasso, ellipse, range, quadrant (with arms that move
one by one) and FlowJo's curly quadrant; boolean combinations. Biexponential, Logicle,
arcsinh, logarithmic and linear scales. Compensation per sample, edited by hand or computed
from single-stain controls by AutoSpill's method. Spectral unmixing (ordinary or weighted
least squares) with spectra, similarity matrix and complexity index. **Auto…** proposes gates
from the data and fits them again when the population above them changes. Models: cell cycle
(Watson or Dean-Jett-Fox, with aneuploid population, debris and aggregates), proliferation
(FlowJo's indices) and kinetics. Export as a FlowJo workspace or Gating-ML 2.0.

**Statistics.** Count and percentages, and median, mean, geometric mean, SD, CV, robust SD,
robust CV and MAD by FlowJo's definitions. Tests between groups, paired or not, with
corrections; GraphPad Prism files (`.pzfx`).

**Explore and Clusters.** UMAP, tSNE, PCA, FlowSOM and PhenoGraph-style clustering, in the
background with FlowJo's defaults. The map, heatmap, FlowSOM tree and frequencies linked;
clusters renamed, merged and turned into populations of the full files. Differential
abundance (diffcyt's DA-edgeR) and differential expression (diffcyt's DS-limma).

**Figures.** Any plot as a figure in millimetres, with editable text, as SVG, PDF, PNG or
TIFF; gates drawn with their names and shares; plates of several panels at a journal's column
width; a PDF report stating the quality control, sampling plan, seeds and settings, and the
version that wrote it.

**The manual.** Help ▸ Manual, or F1 for the page about where you are: every tab and dialog
described, in English and in Portuguese, searchable, and exportable as one PDF.

**The command line.** Everything the window does, without the window: `milc --help`.

## How it was checked

Against FlowJo on real workspaces (gate counts, statistics, scales, FlowSOM), and against the
reference packages — flowCore and FlowUtils, AutoSpill, PeacoQC and flowAI, umap-learn and
openTSNE, FlowSOM, PhenoGraph, edgeR and limma. What was compared, on what and by how much
they agree is in `docs/comparacao.md` and in the manual's *Measured facts* page.
